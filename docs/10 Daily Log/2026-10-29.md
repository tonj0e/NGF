- Android app v1.1 (versionCode 2): app name is now **NGF**; permissions (notifications, then location, then "all the time") are asked when the agent first opens the app, not only after attendance; notifications asked first because the "all the time" step opens system settings and may never call back.
- Route map (`components/routes/RouteMap.tsx`): the Google Maps script now retries up to 3 times (1.5 s, 3 s, 4.5 s) before falling back to the drawing; one phone showed "Not a map" after a single dropped load.
- Android app v1.2: launcher name was still "NGF Agent" (activity label `title_activity_main`); now NGF.
- Verification form drafts (`lib/verificationDraft.ts`, `components/pages/VerificationsPage.tsx`): typed details and chosen photos are saved on the phone (IndexedDB, one draft per agent per lead) while typing/choosing, restored when the lead is opened again (after Back, closing the app, restart), shown as "Saved on this phone until it is sent" and a "Draft saved" tag in the queue; deleted only after the server confirms the submit. Failed submit keeps the draft. The customer OTP check is not stored: it has to be done again after reopening. Works in the website, installed web app and Android app.
- Submit later (red network): pressing Submit with a red light (poor/offline) no longer tries; a pop-up ("Network is too weak to submit") offers **Submit later** (draft saved with `pending` flag, form closes) or stay. `components/agent/PendingSubmitPrompt.tsx` (mounted in AppShell for agents) shows "Network is good now" with one **Submit now** button per pending customer once the light is orange/green (re-asks at most every 10 min; also a service-worker notification in a normal browser when the page is hidden); Submit now opens that lead's form via My Route (`ngf_open_verify` + `ngf:openverify`). Android app v1.3 (versionCode 4): the page tells the native plugin how many are pending (`setPending`); `TrackerService` posts a notification "Network is good now" when a location send answers in under 2.5 s, at most every 15 min (needs attendance/tracking on; the service is not running otherwise). Draft helpers: `pendingDrafts`, `pending`/`name` on `Draft`.
