- Verification form drafts (`lib/verificationDraft.ts`, `components/pages/VerificationsPage.tsx`): typed details and chosen photos are saved on the phone (IndexedDB, one draft per agent per lead) while typing/choosing, restored when the lead is opened again (after Back, closing the app, restart), shown as "Saved on this phone until it is sent" and a "Draft saved" tag in the queue; deleted only after the server confirms the submit.
