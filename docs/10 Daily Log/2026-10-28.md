- Agent live location stopped until the app was closed and reopened. Causes found in `lib/agentTracker.ts`: the phone's location feed (`watchPosition`) often stalls silently (screen off, an error, weak signal) and nothing restarted it; and a send that never got an answer kept `sending = true` so no later send ever went out. Fixes: a 5 s watchdog restarts the feed when no position has come for 25 s; the feed also restarts 3 s after any location error, when the screen comes back (also re-checks attendance with the server) and when the internet returns; each send gives up after 15 s; an old position (over 90 s) is never sent as if live. Limit: while the phone has the app fully in the background/locked, iPhone/Android may still pause it; it recovers by itself when the agent opens the app again.
