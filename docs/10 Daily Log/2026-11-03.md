## Android app: Navigate button showed "No internet"
- Cause: My Route "Navigate" is a normal Google Maps web link. In the Android app (WebView) Google hosts were allowed to open inside the app, the page failed and the app's offline screen ("No internet") appeared.
- Fix (server side, works with the installed app): `lib/openMaps.ts` (`openMapsClick`) — inside the app Navigate opens the Google Maps app with `google.navigation:q=lat,lng&mode=d`; browsers still use the https link. Used in `MyRoutePage.tsx` (lead and subroute stops).
- `android-app/capacitor.config.json`: removed Google hosts from `allowNavigation` so the next APK build also hands them to the phone. 
- "Start from My Route" on the Verification Queue is only a label: a lead in today's route is started from My Route (Navigate → I have reached → Start verification).
